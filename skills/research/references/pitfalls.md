# Pitfalls: how a collection goes wrong, and what this skill cannot settle

Full mode. Load once the collection is running and you are deciding what a
document is worth.

## Failure modes

- **Stacked evidence.** Only material supporting the emerging picture gets
  collected, and the collection looks thorough. Named, with primacy of print
  and false authority, in AFH 33-337, *The Tongue and Quill*, chapter 5.
- **Memory laundering.** Note with no document behind it, sourced to general
  knowledge. Every figure looks sourced.
- **Circular reporting read as corroboration.** references/sources.md.
- **The self-portrait taken as fact.** Originator's own account of itself
  entered without the discount it needs.
- **Confidence growing with volume.** Covered in the skill under when to stop.
- **The vivid case beating the aggregate.** references/sources.md.

## Stated limits

Holes, named so nothing is quietly asserted.

- **The stopping trigger.** Design decision. The chapter cited in the skill
  warns about diminishing returns and never says how to recognize the minimum
  for an informed judgment.
- **Own words.** No check catches a paraphrase that is really a copy without
  the source open beside it. A rule, not a check.
- **When a copy stands in for an original.** No standard here settles when a
  copy is acceptable. references/refused.md.
- **Quoting only from the stored copy.** Convention, not a sourced rule. Here
  because a quotation taken from a summary is a quotation of the summarizer.
- **One fact per row.** Format decision. The rule to transform rather than
  copy, and the three kind marks, are sourced. The row schema is not.
- **Whether an originator is reliable in general.** No adjudicator. Name the
  absence, write down the standard you used, mark it as your own.
- **Where the mode line falls.** The three modes are a design decision. Nothing
  in the documents behind this skill says how much rigor a given question is
  worth. Escalation rule is the guard: when the answer starts wanting a
  judgment the sources do not settle, the run was a full one.
